MECHANISM-BASED EXPECTATION
The anchor removes stalls caused by a frozen goal_ori; on bi_s0 those were joint-5
pins.  c2m512_s1 has the same mechanism in far fewer episodes: its blocked class is
7.5x smaller in FP32 (0.42 vs 3.17%) and 35x smaller in INT8 (0.17 vs 5.92%).
Recovering 95% of the joint-5 cases, as on bi_s0, gives about +0.25 FP32, +0.08 INT8.

PREDICTED DELTA (eps 1e-6 minus eps 0, 12 seeds x 100, paired)
  random FP32    +0.0 to +0.4 pts  (point ~+0.25; upper bound = all 5 blocked recovered)
  random INT8    +0.0 to +0.2 pts  (point ~+0.08; upper bound = both blocked recovered)
  fixed  FP32     0.0              (no failures to recover)
  fixed  INT8    +0.0 to +0.2      (2 failures)
Expected |delta| <= 1.0 pt in every cell, t(11) not significant, and the headline
figures unchanged within noise.  Any gained episodes should be concentrated in the
probe-blocked set.  Changes in other episodes can move either way; the anchor alters
orientation handling in every carry, so a small number of flips outside the class is
expected in both directions.

WHAT WOULD FALSIFY IT
  * a gain > +1.0 pt in any cell, or gained episodes mostly outside the blocked set:
    the anchor acts through something other than the blocked class on this artifact
  * a significant loss: the anchor harms a policy that does not pin
