Grasp checkpoints/td3_grasp_rand_td3_ln_c2m512_s1/best + place
checkpoints/td3_place/best, bread, NATIVE random spawn (position + rotation),

Protocol: 12 seeds x 100 = 1,200 episodes, end-to-end through fsm_sim.py

respawn-and-retry loop resets up to 8 times, so this cannot be recovered from the

matches desktop TFLite EXACTLY on all 14 outputs (boot self-test), INT8 scores

pipeline is therefore representative of the board, and HIL would cap the sample
at ~20 episodes instead of 1,200. Cite this figure as the deployed POLICY STACK
in simulation, not as an on-device measurement.

PRECISION, SEEDS, AND WHY 95.67% IS NOT THE 95.33% HEADLINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Asked of this file: which configuration is "dep"?  Fully: eval_spawn_map_job.sh
(committed bdd5f7a) is the single source -- grasp
checkpoints/td3_grasp_rand_td3_ln_c2m512_s1/best, place checkpoints/td3_place/best,
--object-type bread, --regrasp --rc-steps 60 --rot-anchor-eps 0
--success-criterion robosuite, native random spawn (fsm_sim's default; --fixed-spawn
opts out), rule layer at fsm_sim.py's module defaults.

PRECISION: FP32.  fsm_sim.py loads the Torch actor_td3 weights, so this figure is
the FP32 host pipeline.  It is NOT the INT8 TFLite artifact.

SEEDS: the 12 PROTOCOL seeds (7 31 47 89 101 123 211 307 401 503 555 2024), which
include the 4 that other studies use for selection.  Not a held-out set.

So 95.67% here and the 95.33% in Results/hil_hardware_validation.txt /
Results/critic_capacity_2m.txt are DIFFERENT MEASUREMENTS and must not be presented
as the same one or as a replication:
    95.33%  c2m512_s1 INT8, held out on 12 seeds never used for selection
    94.92%  the same, FP32
    95.67%  this run: FP32, anchor OFF, 12 protocol seeds
They differ in precision, in anchor state, and in seed set.  The agreement to
within ~0.3-0.8 points is reassuring about the pipeline but is NOT a validation of
one by the other.  For a like-for-like comparison with 95.33% the run would have to
be repeated on the INT8 artifact over the same held-out seeds.
