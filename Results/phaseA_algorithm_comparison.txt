FINDINGS
1. SAC sets a new record: 73.3% vs the prior artifact's 46.7% on the same
   full-box test (+26.6 points), and 82% peak rolling success in training.
2. EVERY algorithm decayed after peaking (-20 to -38 points). The decay is a
   property of the task/regime, not of TD3's update rule.
3. PPO decayed too (-31) despite having NO replay buffer. This FALSIFIES
   stale-replay-composition as the mechanism -- the last surviving hypothesis
   from the earlier campaign. On-policy data does not prevent it.
4. Mitigations rank by decay magnitude: SAC (-20) < TD3+LN (-28) < PPO (-31)
   < TD3 (-38). Entropy regularization and LayerNorm both help; neither cures.
5. TD3 collapsed catastrophically: 43% -> 5%, with timeout_no_reach 148/200 in
   the final window -- it forgot how to REACH, not merely how to grasp.
6. Best-checkpoint governance paid for itself: SAC ended training at 62% but
   the preserved best/ evaluates at 73.3%. The decay cost nothing.

CAVEATS
- Single seed. Re-run the top candidates at seeds 1 and 2 before publishing.
- 30-episode evaluation: standard error ~8 points. Use 100+ for thesis numbers.
- Phase B (adding z-rotation, spawn level 2.0) is not yet run.
