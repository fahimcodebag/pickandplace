================================================================================
THE RESIDUAL CORRECTOR MUST RUN IN FP32 ON THE MCU -- INT8 DESTROYS IT
================================================================================
The policy quantises for free (INT8 95.33% vs FP32 94.92% on random spawn,
after weight clipping and in-loop QAT). The corrector does not, and the reason
is instructive.

MEASURED, per-tensor INT8, same path as the policy (--per_tensor, ESP-NN
requires it), calibrated on the real 2181-detection dataset:

  variant                          INT8 vs FP32 output   note
  standardisation FOLDED IN            13.18 mm median   WORSE than the 11.56 mm
                                                          residual it corrects
  standardisation ON DEVICE             6.36 mm median   over half of it
  FP32                                   0.53 mm         the actual correction

WHY. The 12 features span a 2,198x range of scales:
    cx        sd 150.25        det_z     sd 0.068
    area_px   sd  96.72        cam_range sd 0.084
Per-tensor INT8 sets ONE scale for the whole input tensor, so the small
features are crushed by the large ones. Folding the standardisation into
layer 0 made it worse still, pushing that layer's max/std from 3.95 to 5.97 --
the same dynamic-range mechanism documented in
Results/weight_range_regularisation, now on an input tensor rather than weights.

Standardising on the device (24 flops) halves the damage but does not fix it:
6.36 mm of quantisation error against a 11.56 mm residual leaves ~45% of the
correction, versus 95% in FP32.

THE ANSWER IS NOT TO QUANTISE IT
  weights        5,187 params x 4 B = 20.3 KB float32
  free heap      253 KB measured on device
  timing         0.26 ms of a 50 ms budget already used -- 192x headroom
  ESP32          has a single-precision FPU
Quantisation exists to buy memory and compute. Here there is nothing to buy:
20 KB is 8% of free heap, and a 5k-MAC float forward pass is negligible beside
the 0.26 ms the two INT8 policies already take.

This is a genuine asymmetry worth stating: INT8 was NECESSARY for the policy
(to fit at all) and FREE (after clipping + QAT); for the corrector it is
UNNECESSARY and EXPENSIVE. "Quantise everything" is the wrong default.

STATUS
Conversion tooling and the measurement are committed. The firmware integration
(a third model, plus a message type carrying 12 features instead of the object
pose block) is NOT yet built.
