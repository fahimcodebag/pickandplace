================================================================================
CAPACITY: WIDER ACTOR AND LARGER CRITIC BOTH FAIL TO HELP
Neither is a clean negative. Seed variance dwarfs both effects.
================================================================================

MOTIVATION
v7 (2.2M params) certifies 98.7% of random-spawn grasps against the decomposed
stage's 79.2%, so capacity plainly CAN solve this sub-task. Two ways to add it
were tested on top of the best configuration (wclip 8 + in-loop QAT).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXPERIMENT 1: NET2WIDER 128/64 ACTOR  --  FAILED (-5.93 INT8), CONFOUNDED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
net2wider.py transfers the trained 64/32 policy into 128/64 exactly (function
preserved to 1e-13 in double; 3e-5..1e-4 in float32 storage). Arms:
  wide128_sN  widen qat8_sN, train 6000
  cont64_sN   continue qat8_sN at 64/32, train 6000   <- isolates WIDTH

  INT8  77.65% vs 83.58%  -5.93  t=-5.32 p<0.0001  5/6 seeds down
  FP32  80.46% vs 84.71%  -4.25  t=-4.69 p<0.0001  4/6 seeds down

CONFOUND, AND IT IS MINE. Critics were left at 64/32, so actor/critic capacity
went 0.96 -> 2.64 while v7 sits at 1.00. TD3's actor ascends the CRITIC's Q
estimate, so a bigger actor mainly gets better at exploiting a fixed critic's
errors. The experiment tested "wider actor against an unchanged critic", not
"more capacity". The wider net also needed ~2.3x more episodes to reach the
same 95% target (median 3900 vs 1700), the expected sample-efficiency cost of
2.8x parameters on an unchanged data budget.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE THAT MATTERS. Textbook Net2Wider divides outgoing weights
equally by the replication count. Measured: duplicated units then have
identical activations AND identical outgoing weights, so they receive identical
gradients and stay bit-for-bit tied -- divergence after 400 updates was
0.000000, i.e. the added width is unusable. The paper's remedy (weight noise)
destroys the transfer: 0.5% noise moved the output by 0.46 against a 0.1
exploration scale. Splitting the outgoing weights UNEQUALLY instead preserves
the function exactly (shares sum to 1) while giving duplicates different
gradients: divergence 0.000121. Exact transfer and usable capacity.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXPERIMENT 2: 512/256 CRITICS, ACTOR UNCHANGED  --  NO RELIABLE EFFECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Inverts the confound: actor stays at the deployed 64/32 (8088 B INT8,
unchanged), critics get 28.6x capacity (5,569 -> 159,233 params). Critics never
leave the host (Sec 7), so this is free at deployment. BOTH arms initialise
critics fresh so that SIZE is the only variable.

  tseed   big INT8  small INT8       d
  s0        90.33%      82.75%   +7.58
  s1        83.08%      85.25%   -2.17
  s2        79.58%      74.33%   +5.25
  s3        85.08%      70.17%  +14.92
  s4        51.50%      75.00%  -23.50
  s5        63.50%      93.83%  -30.33
  mean      75.51%      80.22%   -4.71

  INT8  t=-2.27 p=0.0265  BUT wilcoxon p=0.0989 (ns)  pairs 36/35
  FP32  t=-3.52 p=0.0008      wilcoxon p=0.0065       pairs 27/41

READ THE DISAGREEMENT. The t-test and Wilcoxon diverge because the mean is
carried by two collapsed cells (big s4 51.50%, s5 63.50%), not by a consistent
shift -- the pair split is 36/35, a coin flip. With outliers this severe the
t-test is not trustworthy. The honest verdict is NO RELIABLE EFFECT from critic
capacity, not a negative one.

BOTH ARMS LOST TO THE INCUMBENT
  cont64   INT8 83.58%   (critics warm-started)
  smallc64 INT8 80.22%   -3.36  (critics fresh, same size)
  bigc512  INT8 75.51%   -8.07  (critics fresh, 28.6x)
Re-initialising critics cost more than critic capacity could buy. Mid-run both
arms collapsed from ~90% grasp to 14-73% once a random critic began driving
actor updates, and spent thousands of episodes recovering. The comparison is
therefore "recovery from a fresh critic", not "improvement over the best
policy". A rerun should Net2Wider the critics from the existing 64/32 ones
rather than starting them from scratch.

UNDERPOWERED FOR THE BIG CRITIC SPECIFICALLY. A 159k-parameter critic was given
the same ~4000-episode budget as a 5.5k one. Bigger function approximators need
more data; at this budget it is very likely underfit, so "no benefit at 4000
episodes" is the correct scope of this result.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE CAMPAIGN'S BEST CELLS NOW CLEAR 90% INT8
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  smallc64_s5   INT8 93.83%   FP32 91.83%
  bigc512_s0    INT8 90.33%   FP32 87.17%
Both are best-of-6 selections. The wclip8_s0 precedent (+6.33 selected ->
+2.92 held-out) says such maxima roughly halve. Held-out validation pending;
until it lands these are candidates, not results.

CONCLUSION
Neither capacity route helped, and neither test was clean. What dominates both
experiments is seed-level instability: INT8 outcomes span 51.50% to 93.83%
across nominally identical configurations. Until that variance is understood or
reduced, capacity ablations at n=6 cannot resolve effects of this size.
