tag16h5 AS THE DEPLOYMENT PATH
==============================
Model: checkpoints/td3_grasp_rand_td3_ln_c2m512_s1/best (FP32 grasp actor)
Camera: robot0_eye_in_hand, 320x240. Latch mode, --detect-until-first, random spawn.
Protocol: 12 seeds x 50 episodes = 600 episodes per row, paired on seed.

END-TO-END TASK SUCCESS
-----------------------
  route                        seeds   ep   mean     median
  agentview 36h11 + corrector    12    600  89.67%   90.00%   (external camera; NOT on-device)
  wrist 16h5   + corrector       12    600  87.67%   87.00%   <-- deployment path
  wrist 36h11  + corrector       12    600  86.83%   87.00%

  Paired, 16h5 - 36h11 on the wrist: +0.83 pts, SE 0.87, t=0.96,
  95% CI [-1.08, +2.74]. The two families are EQUIVALENT end-to-end.

DETECTION LAYER (n=3200 resets per family, 4 seeds x 800)
---------------------------------------------------------
  family   detect rate   raw pose error
  16h5        68.3%         20.8 mm
  36h11       66.9%         20.7 mm
  Also equivalent.

  CORRECTION: an earlier n=30 spot check suggested 16h5 detected 23 points
  better (80% vs 57%). That was sampling noise and is retracted. This is the
  third small-sample over-read in this campaign (see the methods note below).

WHY SWITCH, THEN
----------------
Not accuracy -- flash. The ESP32 AprilTag port (stnk20, esp-idf) ships the
tag16h5 and tag25h9 code tables. tag36h11 has 587 codes and would have to be
added to flash. Since the families perform identically at both the detection
and task level, 16h5 is free.

CORRECTOR
---------
  fit_wrist16_corrector.py, 4 seeds x 800 resets -> 2186 detections.
  Seeds split TUNE (1640) / HOLD (546); features derived from the DETECTED
  pose only, never ground truth.

  config     params   held-out    cut   R2 x/y/z
  h32 d2      1,571     0.43mm    98%   +0.99/+0.99/+1.00
  h64 d2      5,187     0.39mm    98%   +0.99/+0.99/+1.00   <-- selected
  h64 d3      9,347     0.40mm    98%   +0.99/+0.99/+1.00
  held-out baseline 20.65 mm

  h32 is within 0.04 mm at 1/3 the size; h64 chosen only because 20.3 KB is
  immaterial against the 320 KB free measured on hardware. If flash ever gets
  tight, h32 is a free swap.

  Why the residual is this correctable: the detection happens at t=0 with the
  arm at its home pose, so the camera pose is FIXED and detected->true is one
  deterministic calibration map, not a pose-varying function.

EXPORT VALIDATION
-----------------
  corrector_model.h, 5,187 params, 20.3 KB, FP32.
  The mm->m rescale IS applied to the final layer (fit_wrist16_corrector.py
  inherits load()'s *1000 scaling; omitting this rescale previously scored
  0.0% on all 12 seeds).
    magnitude ratio vs true residual: 0.999
    Python, in-sample:                20.76 -> 0.31 mm (median)
    C loop over the header, n=2186:   21.36 -> 0.332 mm (mean)
  The C check compiles corrector_model.h and runs the same arithmetic the
  sketch runs. It is the guard against the unit bug recurring.

  FP32 on purpose: per-tensor INT8 costs 6.4-13.2 mm against a residual whose
  whole magnitude is 20.7 mm (Results/corrector_on_device.txt).

METHODS NOTE -- SMALL-SAMPLE OVER-READS
---------------------------------------
Three claims in this campaign were first reported from small samples and all
three were optimistic:
  wrist accuracy    12.2 mm (n=8)   ->  20.7 mm (n=2140)
  truth ceiling      100%  (n=8)    ->  94.0%  (n=200)
  16h5 detection     +23 pts (n=30) ->  +1.4 pts (n=3200)
The large-sample checks cost minutes each. This is the argument for the
12-seed x 100-episode protocol used for every headline number in this thesis.
