tag16h5 AS THE DEPLOYMENT PATH
==============================
Date: 2026-09-05

WHY 16h5, HONESTLY
------------------
The ESP32 AprilTag port (stnk20/apriltag, esp-idf) ships tag16h5 and tag25h9.
tag36h11's 587-code table is expensive in flash. Adopting 16h5 avoids porting
that table.

It is NOT because 16h5 detects better. An early n=30 spot check suggested 16h5
detected 80% vs 36h11 57% -- a 23-point gap. That was sampling noise. Measured
properly at 4 seeds x 800 resets per family:

  family   detections/3200   rate     raw error median   p90
  36h11         2140         66.9%        20.7 mm       26.5
  16h5          2186         68.3%        20.8 mm       26.5

Equivalent. The switch costs nothing, which is the actual argument for it.

CORRECTOR REFIT (16h5, robot0_eye_in_hand 320x240, t=0)
-------------------------------------------------------
Tag family is part of the per-setup calibration, so the corrector was refit on
16h5 detections. Seeds split TUNE (0,1,2) / HOLD (3).

  config    params    held-out    cut    R2 x/y/z
  h32 d2     1,571     0.43 mm    98%   +0.99/+0.99/+1.00
  h64 d2     5,187     0.39 mm    98%   +0.99/+0.99/+1.00   <- selected
  h64 d3     9,347     0.40 mm    98%   +0.99/+0.99/+1.00

  held-out baseline 20.65 mm

Matches the 36h11 corrector (20.61 -> 0.39 mm) to within noise, as expected:
the residual is a camera-calibration map, not a tag-decoding property.

The final layer was trained in millimetres and rescaled to metres on export
(fit script inherits load()'s *1000). Verified by magnitude ratio against raw
residuals: 0.999. Omitting this rescale previously scored 0.0% on all 12 seeds.

All three routes are statistically indistinguishable. Reference: ground-truth
perception is 94.0%, so the full perception stack costs ~6 points.

The 16h5 wrist route is chosen because it is the only one that FITS: a 320x240
frame is a 75 KB buffer (ESP32 SRAM, 320 KB free after the arena shrink) versus
1200 KB at 1280x960, which needs PSRAM this board does not have.

HEADER VALIDATION
-----------------

Python model over 12 real detections:

  max |C - Python| = 8.3e-08 m (0.083 um)   MATCH

FP32 on purpose: per-tensor INT8 cost 6.4-13.2 mm against the residual it

METHODS NOTE
------------
Three claims in this campaign were first reported from small samples and all
three were optimistic:

  claim              small sample        large sample
  wrist accuracy     12.2 mm (n=8)       20.7 mm (n=2140)
  truth ceiling      100%   (n=8)        94.0%  (n=200)
  16h5 detection     +23 pts (n=30)      +1.4 pts (n=3200)

A fourth was caught before publication: seed 7 in this run read 95% at the
20/50 mark and finished at 86%. This is why headline numbers use the full
12-seed x 50-100 episode protocol.
