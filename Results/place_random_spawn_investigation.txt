================================================================================
RANDOM-SPAWN PLACE TRAINING -- what fails, and what does not
STATUS: INTERIM (2026-09-11).  The controlled curriculum pair and the cereal
from-scratch arm are still training; the correlation report is produced
unattended by finish_curriculum_pair.sh.
================================================================================

QUESTION
Can a place (transport) policy be trained for random spawn -- per object, or at
all?  thesis_context 9.13 records random-spawn transport retraining as a
settled negative (bread, twice, six seeds).  This thread started from a
per-object cereal place policy and ended somewhere else.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PER-OBJECT CEREAL PLACE POLICY LOSES TO NOT TRAINING ONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Warm-started from the bread place actor, default recipe (buffer 200k / batch
512 / critic 64x32, LayerNorm), random spawn, 8000 episodes, 5 seeds.  Final
best/ checkpoints, deterministic through fsm_sim, 12 eval seeds x 50:

    s2 85.33   s3 76.00   s1 71.83   s0 69.17   s4 36.50
    mean 67.77%   sd 18.52
    bread policy transferred (control)   73.33%   pooled -5.57, t(59)=-2.31
    scripted waypoint transport          89.17%

EVALUATION ARTIFACT FOUND ON THE WAY.  A first evaluation read best/ while the
trainers were still overwriting it and reported s0/s1/s4 as 16.33/3.50/1.17%.
Re-run after the trainers exited: 69.17/71.83/36.50%.  The two seeds whose
best/ was already stable came back bit-identical.  Never evaluate checkpoints
owned by a running trainer.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. GRASP-STAGE HYPERPARAMETER LEVERS DO NOT TRANSFER TO PLACE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Warm-started from cereal s2 (85.33%), 3 seeds each, 12 x 50:

    arm        buffer  batch  critic    cereal mean   sd
    default    200k    512    64x32       67.77%     18.52
    big        2M      1024   512x256     35.22%     29.43
    smallbuf   200k    1024   512x256     39.33%      2.03

    smallbuf - big   (buffer alone)        +4.11
    smallbuf - def   (batch + critic)     -28.44

Bread, same big recipe, warm-started CROSS-object from cereal s2: 27.00 / 34.33
/ 11.83, mean 24.39%, against 92.50% for the untouched fixed-spawn original.

The batch/critic change COLLAPSED seed variance (sd 18.52 -> 2.03) almost
exactly as it did on grasp (17.18 -> 2.84) -- onto a mean 28 points worse.  The
mechanism transfers; the benefit inverts.  Together with critic resets (grasp
+20..+30, transport -30; 9.6) this is a second, independent falsified transfer.

BUFFER ARITHMETIC.  The place stage makes ~38-49 transitions per episode
(391,472 at episode 8000), so a 2M buffer fills only at ~40-52k episodes.  At
8000 episodes it is 15-20% full and never cycles.  Correct arithmetic, causally
minor: buffer size alone is worth +4.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WARM ACTOR + COLD CRITIC IS NOT THE CAUSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Actor-only warm starts load no critic and run 10,000 steps of random warmup
before the actor acts.  --warm-start-critics --skip-warmup removes both
(banner: [actor, critics, no-random-warmup]).  Result:
    best/ for s1, s2 byte-identical to the source (never improved on)
    periodic weights at ~2,500 episodes: 10.67 / 42.33 / 12.00% end-to-end
The policy still collapses from 85%.  best/ hid it: the best-selector kept the
initialisation, so earlier "final" figures overstated what training produced.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE CURRICULUM, AND WHY FIXED SPAWN CLIMBS IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Reverse curriculum (Hurdle 5): a scripted carry hands off at frac x distance;
frac advances 0.2 -> 1.0 only on >= 75% success over 40 NOISY training
episodes.  Maximum frac ever reached:
    cereal/pairfix/cerealsmall/cerealbig/breadbig    0.30-0.40
    cerealbig over ~26,000 episodes                  0.30
    9.13 bread attempts                              0.60 / 0.40
    original fixed-spawn run (thesis_context 3)      0.96 at 88%

ZERO-ACTION SCAFFOLDING FLOOR (policy removed, 40 episodes each):
    bread   fixed   frac 0.2  100.0% / 100.0%   frac 0.3  95.0% / 82.5%
    bread   random  frac 0.2   77.5% /  80.0%   frac 0.3  42.5% / 37.5%
                                (random actions / zero actions)
    cereal  random  frac 0.2   37.5%   frac 0.5   0.0%   frac 1.0   0.0%
    source cereal s2 (trained)  87.5%            65.0%              60.0%

At fixed spawn the first rungs are FREE: the curriculum advances before the
policy learns anything.  Mechanism: frac is RELATIVE to each episode's distance
but the release radius is ABSOLUTE (0.10 m).  Fixed spawn's distance is a
constant ~0.37 m, so frac 0.2 = 7.4 cm, always inside the radius.  Random spawn
reaches 0.65 m, so frac 0.2 is up to 13 cm and a third of handoffs start
outside it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. SMALLER DEFECTS, REAL BUT NOT THE CAUSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCRIPTED CARRY PINS JOINT 5.  _scripted_carry_toward_bin builds its action
with np.zeros, so a[3:6] = 0 freezes goal_ori -- the orientation_anchor defect,
inside the curriculum carry.  Bread, random spawn, zero actions:
    eps 0     carry reached stop 36/40, ran out of steps 4, handoff max 0.416 m
    eps 1e-6  carry reached stop 40/40, ran out 0,      handoff max 0.130 m
    success   32 -> 33 (frac 0.2)   15 -> 18 (frac 0.3)
NOT yet fixed in place_env_wrapper.py: the carry and the release routine still
write a[3:6] = 0.

TRAIN / DEPLOY MISMATCH.
    constant               training wrapper   deployed FSM
    release radius              0.10              0.18
    release hold steps             5                 3
    translation scale            0.5              0.65
    RECENTER phase               none            60 steps
Same weights (cereal s2): 60% at full distance in the wrapper, 85.33% through
the FSM.  The original fixed-spawn model trained under the same mismatch and
deploys at 93.58%, so it is survivable -- but it is a defect.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. WHAT A COLLAPSED PLACE POLICY ACTUALLY DOES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AT THE ZERO-ACTION FLOOR, AT EVERY DISTANCE (cereal, random spawn, pinned frac):
                     frac 0.2   0.5    1.0
    zero actions       37.5     0.0    0.0
    source s2          87.5    65.0   60.0
    pairfix s0 final   37.5     0.0    0.0     identical to zeros
    pairfix s1 final   45.0     5.0    2.5
    pairfix s2 final   57.5    12.5    0.0
Not curriculum forgetting (that predicts short-hop skill kept); the policy is
nullified even at the difficulty it trained on.

NOT FREEZING.  On 710 states visited by the working policy:
                  |a_xy|  saturated  toward bin
    source s2      0.768     29.9%      97%
    pairfix s0     1.138     55.3%      31%
    pairfix s1     0.649     16.9%      73%
    pairfix s2     1.193     65.7%     100%
Collapsed actors command harder, not less, in seed-dependent directions.

DROPS DO NOT PRECEDE IT.  dropped + fell_off_table stayed at 0-1 per 50 while
success fell from ~41 to 11-28 in the first ~400 episodes.

NOT A KINEMATIC BLOCK.  Commanded vs achieved motion, last 30 steps before
termination, full distance:
                            track   cos(a, to bin)   obj z   stall distance
    source s2  success      23.3%       +0.74        1.614       0.131
    source s2  stall        25.0%       -0.39        1.549       0.223
    pairfix s2 stall        24.2%       -0.18        1.097       0.489
    pairfix s0 stall        24.8%       -0.62        1.281       0.749
Tracking is ~24% for everyone, including successes.  What separates them is
DIRECTION near termination.  pairfix s2 points toward the bin on 100% of the
source's states but away on its own terminal states: self-induced distribution
shift, the classic off-policy actor-critic failure.  Collapsed policies carry
lower and stall far from the bin.

NO COLLISION.  0.0% agent-environment contact in every outcome class, with a
validated detector (section 8).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. THE CONTROLLED PAIR -- AND WHY "RANDOM SPAWN FAILS" IS NOT ESTABLISHED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every collapsed run above was CEREAL, WARM-STARTED, or both.  The curriculum
pair removes both: bread, from scratch, default recipe, same grasp policy,
same seeds, ONLY --random-spawn differs, snapshots every 250 episodes.

LEARNING-GATE TIMING CONFOUND.  td3.py learn() is a no-op until
mem_cntr >= batch_size * 10 = 5,120; the actor acts only once time_step >=
10,000.  Fixed-spawn episodes are short (the scaffolding finishes them), so:
    curF_s0  ep 500  mem_cntr 3,620   actor = seeded init
    curR_s2  ep 500  time_step 13,112 already acting
Snapshots at ep 250/500 were byte-identical within a seed and ACROSS arms at
the same seed -- the initialisation.  Compare arms on time_step, not episode.

INTERIM TRAJECTORY (success per 50, curF ep ~2,100 / curR ep ~1,570):
  * Every collapse, in BOTH arms, immediately follows a curriculum advance --
    the Hurdle 5 limit cycle at ~0.40, reproduced.
      curF_s1  frac 0.40 at ep 1200 -> 8, 0, 0, 4 -> frac REGRESSED -> 32-37
      curF_s2  50/50 x6 at 0.40 -> 0.45 -> 20,16,20,2,20,5 -> recovering to 23
      curR_s1  0.30 -> 20 ... 9 -> recovers 43-45 -> advances to 0.35
  * No between-arm gap: recent fixed seeds 23-50, random seeds 27-46.
Fixed spawn climbs faster because its early rungs are free, and so reaches the
hard 0.40-0.45 zone first.  Nothing yet shows random spawn cannot learn
transport.

CORRELATION (training metric vs end-to-end), interim: fixed pooled n=9
trained snapshots, place50 Spearman +0.13, frac50 +0.15, metric +0.14 -- too
few to read.  Final: Results/curriculum_pair/correlation_report.txt.

CEREAL FROM-SCRATCH ARM (td3_place_cerealscratch_s0-2, launched 2026-09-11):
curR with one change, the object.  Collapses -> the failure is cereal.
Healthy -> the failure was warm-starting.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. CORRECTIONS MADE DURING THIS THREAD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* CONTACT DETECTOR.  Filtering geom names on "bin" matches only the visual legs;
  the colliding walls and floor are unnamed.  A box resting on the bin floor
  gave 4 contacts and zero hits.  Classifying by parent body fixes it
  (validated: registers place_bin|object).  Consequence for
  orientation_anchor.txt: its "contacts NONE" row was unsupported when written;
  RE-MEASURED at eps 0 it holds -- 2,802 stalled steps, 100% joint-pinned,
  0.0% contact -- and eps 1e-6 removes stalls entirely (16/40 -> 33/40).
* OBJECT MISMATCH.  A bread zero-action floor (80%) was compared against cereal
  runs to claim "learning is worse than doing nothing".  Cereal's floor is
  37.5%; the claim is withdrawn.
* ETA.  A place-training ETA extrapolated from early, mostly-failing episodes
  (4 h) was too short: successes run the full place phase, so rising success
  lowers episodes per minute.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. LEDGER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REFUTED            buffer size as the cause; batch/critic transfer (harmful);
                   warm actor + cold critic / random warmup; curriculum
                   forgetting; "worse than zero actions" (object mismatch);
                   policy freezing; drops preceding collapse; kinematic block;
                   bin collision; scripted-carry pin as the primary cause
ESTABLISHED        cereal per-object place training loses to transfer;
                   grasp levers do not transfer to place; curriculum never
                   advances in the collapsed runs; fixed spawn's early rungs
                   are free (relative frac vs absolute radius); collapsed
                   policies sit at the zero-action floor and fail by
                   self-induced distribution shift; learning-gate timing
                   confound between spawn conditions
NOT SUPPORTED YET  "random spawn itself breaks place training" -- the only
                   controlled comparison shows no gap
OPEN               cereal vs warm-start (arm running); what drives the
                   self-induced shift; robosuite built-in reward never tried
                   on place (see 10); wrapper scripted phases still write
                   a[3:6] = 0; train/deploy constant mismatch

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. BUILT-IN REWARD ON THE PLACE STAGE -- NEVER TRIED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
train_place.py constructs PickPlace with reward_shaping=False and replaces it
with the custom potential (thesis_context 3, Hurdle 1).  robosuite's
staged_rewards() was adopted only for GRASP (9.9, 79.2 -> 87.1% certified).
It is dense and includes an approach term: r_hover rises toward 0.7 as the
lifted object nears the bin.  Two risks to price before training (9.10):
  * r_hover is a STATE reward paid every step over the bin -- the Hurdle 1
    hover exploit that moved this stage to potential shaping.
  * the grasp version scored "never gripped" second-best until an idle cost
    was added, because terminating early forfeits remaining per-step income.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Evaluate checkpoints only after the owning trainer exits, or from write-once
  snapshots (train_place.py --snapshot-every writes ep_XXXXX.tmp then renames).
* Key cross-condition comparisons on training progress (time_step), not
  episode index, whenever episode length depends on the condition.
* Give every detector a positive control before trusting a zero.
* When comparing against a floor or baseline, check it is the same object.
* Background jobs: identify by /proc comm + cmdline, never pgrep -f alone.
