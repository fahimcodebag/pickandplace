episodes (thesis_context 9.18; the one ~26,000-episode run is the exception -- see
the correction in place_random_spawn_investigation.txt section 4).
Three batches test three responses:
  A   custom reward + EARLY STOPPING on periodic end-to-end evaluation
  B'  robosuite's built-in staged reward used as a POTENTIAL, plus success
  C'  B' with the shaping annealed toward sparse once training success builds up
  (B and C were first planned as bare built-in and built-in minus an idle cost;
  gate 2 below showed both mispriced, and the user approved B' and C' instead on
  2026-09-12.)

DECISIONS (from the user, 2026-09-11)
  object        cereal, random spawn
  seeds         6 per batch: 3 from scratch + 3 warm-started (seeds 10 11 12 each)
  stopping      early stopping in batch A only; B and C run the full 4000 episodes
                and are scored from snapshots afterwards
  "fixes"       the user chose "the one that worked best".  Read as the IDLE COST:
                it is the fix that made built-in reward work on the grasp stage
                (79.2 -> 87.1% certified, thesis_context 9.9) and it targets the
                trap built-in reward sets under this stage's termination (success
                ends the episode and forfeits per-step income).  Its size is set by

DESIGN (run_place_es_batch.sh)
  recipe        as run_cereal_scratch.sh: buffer 200k, batch 512, critic 64x32,
                LayerNorm, 4000 episodes, snapshots every 250, cereal_alignwarm_s0
                grasp, curriculum on, ROT_ANCHOR_EPS in the wrapper (training value)
  warm start    actor only from td3_place_cerealscratch_s2/best (89.00% on 8 held-out
                seeds x 100), --skip-warmup.  Its critic learned the custom reward,
                so B and C cannot reuse it; A starts the same way so the batches
                differ only in reward and stopping.
  early stop    es_watch.py scores every snapshot through fsm_sim.py (seeds 7 31 x
                50, anchor 1e-6, robosuite criterion, settle 60).  Best = highest
                success, ties to the earliest.  STOP once 4 later snapshots fail to
                beat it and the latest is at episode >= 1000.  The selected policy is
                the best snapshot.  Early stopping does not change learning; it
                decides when to stop and which snapshot to keep.
  B'            reward_mode builtin_potential: r = 0.99*Phi(s') - Phi(s) + 1.0 once on
                success, Phi = largest robosuite staged term.  Holding still earns
                -(1-0.99)*Phi <= 0, so the stall trap cannot exist.  Success is the
                wrapper's object-aware check (robosuite's own fails for a flat cereal
                box); once placed, Phi is held at its maximum 0.7, because robosuite
                zeroes the staged terms for a placed object and would otherwise charge
                the policy for succeeding.
  C'            B' + train_place.py --anneal-shaping: reward = w*shaping + success.
                w = 1.0 until training place success over 200 episodes reaches 60%,
                then falls linearly to 0.1 over 1000 episodes, never rising.  Shaping
                and success are stored per transition beside the replay buffer, and
                every sampled batch is RE-SCORED at the current w, so the critic never
                learns from a mix of reward functions.  Control: at w = 1 the
                re-scored reward must equal the stored one (checked on 50 batches).
                The trigger is training success, which the curriculum also moves.
                CONTROL COVERAGE: the w = 1 re-scoring control printed in all three
                C' SCRATCH runs (max |stored - re-scored| = 0.00e+00).  The three warm
                runs annealed at episode 200, before 50 sampling batches had
                accumulated, so the control never printed there -- same code path,
                verified on the scratch runs, but not independently checked in the
                warm ones.  Determinism check: the two C' runs whose anneal never
                triggered (scr_s10, scr_s11) are bit-identical to their B' twins.
  reward modes  place_env_wrapper.py reward_mode: custom (default, unchanged) |
                builtin (robosuite PickPlace.reward() with shaping, at each
                transition's end state) | builtin_idle (builtin minus idle_cost per
                policy step).  The custom reward is always computed: termination
                reads its counters.

essentially never seen the real task. Peak-snapshot selection beat best/ in all
four runs. This extends the +34/+33 point selection gaps recorded in section 5
(9.18, Results/place_selection) to a case where best/ is not merely late but
catastrophic.

THE BUFFER HYPOTHESIS IS REFUTED.  This batch existed because the 2M recipe had
only ever been judged at 8,000 episodes, where the buffer is 15-20% full and never
cycles. Measured fill points, from the trainers' own checkpoint lines:
    noise_s20  2,000,000 at ~ep 30,000   -> 25,000 episodes on a full buffer
    noise_s21  2,000,000 at ~ep 33,500   -> 21,500
    noise_s22  2,000,000 at ~ep 35,000   -> 20,000
    plain_s20  2,000,000 at ~ep 36,000   -> 19,000
So each run trained 19,000-25,000 episodes (~40% of its life) on a full, cycling
buffer, and none of them improved on its initialisation. The best checkpoint in
the batch is at episode 4,000, LONG BEFORE fill. Caveat on the mechanism: at ~53
transitions per episode a full 2M buffer holds ~38,000 episodes of history, so
"full" also means the earliest easy-curriculum experience is being evicted FIFO --
capacity and recency change together, so this refutes the budget argument without
isolating which of the two mattered.
