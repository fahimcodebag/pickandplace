RULE-LAYER SWEEP + A 3-POINT MEASUREMENT CORRECTION
Tuning: +2.25 pts (ns). The correction is the bigger result.
================================================================================

MOTIVATION
Sec 5 of thesis_context recorded that FSM parameters ALONE took end-to-end
78% -> 92% at fixed spawn with identical weights. Those values were never
re-tuned for random spawn, where the grasp stage now reaches transport in
400/400 episodes and every remaining failure is downstream.

STAGE 1 -- coordinate sweep, 25 configs x 100 episodes
Only TRANSLATE_SCALE (multiplier on the transport policy's commanded
translation) showed a coherent trend:
    0.4    0.5(base)  0.6   0.7   0.8   1.0
    80%      82%      86%   86%   85%   83%
Everything else was inside noise. Two informative nulls:
  * PLACE_HORIZON 200 / 400 / 500 all scored 82%. The failing carries are
    STUCK, NOT SLOW -- more time cannot help them.
  * NEAR_TARGET_XY 0.10 cost 11 points: the release trigger never fires.

STAGE 2 -- top candidates, 400 episodes each
All five beat baseline (+1.8 to +3.5) but none individually significant.

CONFIRMATION -- 1200 episodes per arm (12 eval seeds x 100)
  baseline rule layer                                1048/1200  87.33%
  tuned (TSCALE 0.65, CARRY_GAIN 6.0, NEAR_XY 0.18)  1075/1200  89.58%
  difference                          +2.25 pts, z = +1.73, NOT significant

Real but small, and no Sec 5-scale win: the parameters were already close to
right for this regime. Adopted anyway (free, and every stage-2 candidate beat
baseline), but not claimed as significant.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE CORRECTION -- this matters more than the tuning
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The BASELINE, unchanged configuration, scores 87.33% over 1200 episodes against
the 84.0% previously reported from 400. Per-seed, identical config:

  seed    7  31  47  89 101 123 211 307 401 503 555 2024
        82  90  85  89  88  88  88  91  90  91  82   84

Per-seed sd = 3.3 points, so a 4-seed mean carries SE 1.6 -- and the original
draw (7, 123, 555, 2024) took three of the four lowest seeds in the set.

This is the 100-episode lesson one level up. Moving 100 -> 400 episodes fixed
WITHIN-seed noise while keeping only 4 eval seeds, and at that scale
BETWEEN-seed variance dominates. New rule: >= 12 eval seeds x 100 episodes,
not merely >= 400 episodes.

CORRECTED HEADLINE (tuned rule layer, 1200 episodes each)
                    fixed spawn        random spawn
  FP32              100.0% (200 ep)       89.58%
  INT8               96.58%               76.08%
  INT8 cost           ~-3.4                -13.50

Superseded: FP32 random 84.0%, INT8 random 70.0%, INT8 fixed 95.2% -- all from
4-7 eval seeds and low by 3-6 points. Rankings in Results/int8_deployment.txt
are unaffected (all arms measured under the same protocol); only the absolute
values move.
