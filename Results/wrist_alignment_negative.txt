================================================================================
WRIST ALIGNMENT: NEGATIVE RESULT
The term barely moved the mechanism it targeted, and cost flat-grip quality.
gripfix_s2 remains the best artifact.
================================================================================

HYPOTHESIS
The handoff diagnostic established that 24% of grasps close on the object's
corners, that corner grips cost 20-26 points end-to-end across three
independent policies, and that they pass lift certification unpunished. Adding
a wrist-alignment term to the grasp reward should reduce corner prevalence and
move end-to-end success toward gripfix_s2's flat-grip rate of 92.6%.

SETUP
Three seeds, config byte-identical to the gripfix runs with --align-grip added
(success-bonus multiplier in [0.5, 1.0] plus potential-based approach shaping).
All three hit the 85% early-stop target at 4584 / 2111 / 3846 episodes.
Evaluated at 400 end-to-end episodes each (4 eval seeds x 100) through
handoff_diag.py, so one pass yields both outcome and grip angle.

RESULT 1 -- GRASP STAGE UNCHANGED (expected, not the test)
  certified grasp, 800 deterministic episodes
    align   s0/s1/s2   73.0 / 83.1 / 80.4   mean 78.8%
    gripfix s0/s1/s2   78.4 / 80.5 / 78.8   mean 79.2%
A corner grip already passes certification, so this number was never the
target. Flat here is the correct outcome.

RESULT 2 -- THE MECHANISM BARELY FIRED
Mean angle from the nearest flat face (lower = better aligned):
    gripfix_s2  18.9 deg
    align_s0    16.8 deg   p=0.011  (significant, but only 2.1 deg)
    align_s1    18.1 deg   p=0.181  ns
    align_s2    18.2 deg   p=0.307  ns
Corner-grip prevalence (>=30 deg): 23.5% -> 20.5 / 24.5 / 20.5%.
The term was supposed to remove corner grips. It removed ~2 points of them in
one seed of three. Wrist-yaw spread barely changed (sd 19.9 -> 15.1/32.0/18.8),
so the policies did not adopt a meaningfully different rotation strategy.

RESULT 3 -- IT DAMAGED THE GRIPS THAT WERE ALREADY GOOD
  success by grip angle       flat 0-15    mid 15-30   corner 30-45
    gripfix_s2                  92.6%        81.7%        70.2%
    align_s0                    85.8%        78.5%        73.2%
    align_s1                    81.9%        73.5%        63.3%
    align_s2                    85.2%        79.3%        74.4%
Flat-grip success fell 92.6% -> 81.9-85.8% (align_s2 vs gripfix_s2: z=2.25,
p=0.024). Corner-grip success rose slightly in two seeds. The term traded away
quality on the majority case to buy a little on the minority case.

RESULT 4 -- NET END-TO-END IS WORSE
  gripfix_s2   335/400  83.8%
  align_s0     325/400  81.2%   (vs gripfix_s2: z=1.04, ns)
  align_s2     324/400  81.0%   (ns)
  align_s1     301/400  75.2%   (z=3.03, significantly WORSE)
  align mean            79.1%
Two of three seeds are statistically indistinguishable from the incumbent and
one is significantly worse. No seed beats it.

WHY IT PROBABLY FAILED (hypothesis, not established)
The multiplier reduces the success bonus for ANY grip that is not perfectly
flat -- which is most successful grasps. Its intended effect was to
differentiate corner from flat; its actual effect was to weaken the primary
grasp-success signal across the board while offering at most +10 reward units,
discounted, at episode end, against W_GRASP's +10 PER STEP for simply holding
on. The shaping term adds approach-phase gradient but is worth only ~+2. The
incentive to rotate was never competitive with the incentive to grip and hold,
so the policies mostly ignored it and paid the diluted bonus instead.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT OVERTURN
The diagnostic itself still stands: corner grips ARE worse, monotonically, in
all four policies measured here as well (align_s2: 85.2 -> 79.3 -> 74.4). The
finding was correct; this particular intervention did not exploit it.

A future attempt should make alignment a GATE rather than a multiplier -- e.g.
require alignment before the hold counter advances, so a corner grip cannot
certify at all -- rather than competing with W_GRASP on reward magnitude. Not
attempted here.

STATUS
Pipeline best remains gripfix_s2 + ORIGINAL transport. Its grasp rate was
confirmed on held-out eval seeds (2024/555: 79.5% vs 78.8% on the selection
seeds), so it is not a selection artifact.
