================================================================================
FULL PERCEPTION ON A PLAIN ESP32: THE WRIST-CAMERA ROUTE
86.8% end-to-end, one detection per episode, ~100 KB of 253 KB free. No PSRAM.
================================================================================

THE BLOCKER, AND WHAT DISSOLVED IT
On-device AprilTag looked impossible: detection needs the DECODED pixel array
(adaptive threshold, union-find, quad fit all need random pixel access), and a
1280x960 grayscale buffer is 1200 KB against ~253 KB free internal SRAM. JPEG
does not help -- 120 KB compressed still decodes to 1200 KB.

But the binding quantity is TAG PIXELS, not resolution. Measured tag size:

  camera              res        buffer   detected   tag side   verdict
  agentview        1280x960     1200 KB     20/20     26.3 px   needs PSRAM
  agentview         320x240       75 KB      0/20      0.0 px   fits, blind
  eye_in_hand       640x480      300 KB     14/20     41.9 px   needs PSRAM
  eye_in_hand       320x240       75 KB     14/20     19.9 px   FITS, DECODES

The wrist camera sits ~10x closer, so it still resolves a 19.9 px tag at
320x240 -- where agentview at the same resolution sees nothing at all.

RAW ACCURACY IS WORSE, BUT FAR MORE CORRECTABLE
2140 t=0 detections over 3200 resets (67% per attempt):
  raw pose error   median 20.7 mm, p90 26.5    (agentview 1280x960: 11.2 mm)

Corrector refit on THIS setup -- it is a per-setup calibration and the
agentview model does not transfer. Tuned on seeds 0-2, held out on seed 3:

  config     params   held-out   cut    R2 x/y/z
  h32 d2      1,571     0.39mm   98%   +0.99/+0.99/+1.00
  h64 d2      5,187     0.40mm   98%   +0.99/+0.99/+1.00
  h64 d3      9,347     0.40mm   98%   +0.99/+0.99/+1.00

98% against agentview's 94%, from a model a third the size. The reason is
structural: at t=0 the arm is at its home pose, so the camera pose is FIXED and
detected->true is ONE deterministic calibration map. A LINEAR fit on detected
position alone already recovers 94% here, against 7% for agentview, whose
residual varied with arm configuration and occlusion. A fixed viewpoint is the
easiest calibration problem there is.

(Not the spawn prior: that would leave the ~100 mm spread of the spawn box, not
1.22 mm.)

THE STACK THAT NOW FITS
  capture 320x240 grayscale        75 KB
  AprilTag detect                  unmodified library, 19.9 px tag
  corrector MLP                     6.1 KB FP32 (1,571 params)
  grasp + place INT8                16 KB
  ---------------------------------------
  ~100 KB against 253 KB free internal SRAM

No PSRAM, no tiled decoding, no image streaming over serial. One detection per
episode, so a detector taking seconds out of slow memory would still be fine.

CAVEATS
  - 67% detection per attempt. --detect-until-first keeps trying as the arm
    moves (re-rendering an identical frame is deterministic in simulation, so a
    naive retry is useless); measured 1.00 detections per episode in the end.
  - The corrector is a per-setup calibration. Any change of camera, resolution
    or mounting requires refitting (wrist_dataset.py + fit_wrist_corrector.py).
  - AprilTag on the MCU is still an unported library. This establishes that it
    FITS, not that it has been run.
