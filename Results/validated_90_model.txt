--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE UNCOMFORTABLE PART: IT CAME FROM AN ARM REPORTED AS A FAILURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
smallc64 was the CONTROL of the large-critic experiment, arm mean 80.22% INT8,
recorded in Results/capacity_experiments.txt as losing to the incumbent by
-3.36. Its s5 seed scored 93.83%.

Across nominally identical configurations this campaign observed INT8 outcomes
from 51.50% to 93.83%. Arm means are therefore a poor guide to what any single
run produces, and every "this configuration does not help" verdict in this
campaign should be read as a statement about the MEAN, not about the reachable
maximum. Both are legitimate; they answer different questions. A deployment
programme wants the validated maximum, a science question wants the mean.

That variance is the largest unexplained factor in the campaign and is NOT
addressed by anything measured here.

THE QUANTISATION AMPLIFICATION IS GONE
Results/transport_stall_diagnosis recorded that quantisation cost 3.33 points
at the tuned pose and 12.34 under randomisation -- a 3.7x amplification, and
one of the campaign's headline findings. On this artifact:
    fixed spawn   99.83 - 98.67 = +1.16   (was +3.33)
    random spawn  91.67 - 93.08 = -1.41   (was +12.34)
The amplification does not merely shrink, it disappears: INT8 is now BETTER
than FP32 under randomisation. Weight-range clipping and in-loop QAT between
them removed the mechanism that made randomised poses quantise badly.
