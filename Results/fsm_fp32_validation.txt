WHY THIS WAS NEEDED
Every end-to-end number in this project came from test_place.py /
place_env_wrapper.py -- the TRAINING/EVAL wrapper. The artefact that actually
ships is pick_and_place_INT8_FSM.ino. The two are not equivalent, and the
differences were never measured:

  - The sketch has NO transport-stall early termination. place_env_wrapper
    kills a carry after 50 steps without new-best XY progress; the sketch gives
    it the full 300-step PLACE_HORIZON. transport_stall is the dominant
    remaining failure mode, so this alone could move the headline number.
  - GRASP_CAP is 250 in the sketch vs _GRASP_HORIZON 200 in the wrapper.

fsm_sim.py is a host-side FP32 replica of the sketch: all 9 phases, all
constants transcribed verbatim, plus hil_main.py's respawn-and-retry loop and
its grasp/success flag derivation. Only the serial round-trip is removed; the
two FP32 actors run in-process.

SANITY CHECKS ON THE PERFECT SCORE
Median 126 scored steps (min 70, max 145); every episode ran the full pipeline;
mean handoff attempts 1.00, max 1 -- no retries were needed at all. Both eval
seeds returned 100/100 independently. Not a degenerate pass.

FINDING 1 -- THE HARNESSES AGREE
  deployed FSM   336/400  84.0%
  Python wrapper 344/400  86.0%   z = -0.79, ns
The 86.0% figure was not an artefact of the eval wrapper. This was the real
risk being tested, and it is closed.

FINDING 2 -- THE FSM DISCRIMINATES MORE SHARPLY THAN THE WRAPPER
  gap bi_s0 - gripfix_s2:  deployed FSM +7.0 pts   Python wrapper +2.2 pts
The built-in-reward grasp gain is LARGER through the real FSM than through the
eval harness. Most plausibly the sketch's lack of a transport-stall cutoff lets
marginal carries recover, and bi_s0's better grips exploit that where
gripfix_s2's do not. Not verified.

STATUS
FP32 is fully validated end to end. What remains unvalidated is the INT8 step
and hardware: grasp_int8.tflite / place_int8.tflite date from Aug 17 (commit
cbd4535) and predate lift certification, the checkpoint-selection fix, and the
built-in reward. The recorded 90% HIL figure describes different models, on
fixed spawn, over 10 episodes -- a sample size that has run ~10 points
optimistic four times in this project.

Quantization now has a known FP32 reference to measure its drop against:
100.0% fixed / 84.0% random.
