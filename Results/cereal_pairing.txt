READING
  * Scored, the best trained cereal place policy TIES scripted transport (-1.17,
    not significant).  Physically, scripted places better (99.58% vs 96.50%,
    t(11)=-4.54).  Scripting costs zero training; the trained policy took three
    seeds to produce one that works, and that run's final weights score 13.38%.
    So on cereal too a learned transport policy is not required -- now against a
    trained policy, not a transfer.
  * The anchor HELPS this pipeline's place actor (+22.67 trained, +7.75 transfer):
    the opposite sign from c2m512_s1 on bread (-2.75 FP32,
    orientation_anchor_c2m512.txt).  Its effect depends on grasp and object, so
    fsm_sim.py's default (0.0 since 2026-09-11) is right for the deployed
    artifact and wrong for these cells: pass --rot-anchor-eps explicitly.
  * NEAR_TARGET_XY 0.08 is a scripted-path setting.  On the trained place actor it
    costs -21.75, confirming orientation_anchor.txt caveat 3.
