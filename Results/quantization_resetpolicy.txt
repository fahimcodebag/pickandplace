================================================================================
INT8 QUANTIZATION OF THE CRITIC-RESET GRASP POLICY
Source: checkpoints/td3_grasp_rand_td3_ln_resetA_s0/best  (td3_ln + resets, seed 0)
================================================================================

PIPELINE
  QAT   : 50 epochs MSE distillation against the FP32 teacher
          (loss 0.164 -> 0.137; refined-vs-teacher MSE 0.055)
  PTQ   : static INT8, weights PER-TENSOR, calibrated on 4096 real replay
          states drawn from the run's own 200k-transition buffer
  Result: 7.9 KB, tensor dtypes {int8: 8, int32: 3} -- full-integer, int8 I/O

DETERMINISTIC EVALUATION (100 episodes, seed 123, spawn level 1.0)
  precision            success   steps (successes)   steps (all eps)   size
  FP32                  89.0%          26                  45          24.5 KB
  INT8 per-tensor       81.0%          28                  61           7.9 KB
  ---
  prior artifact FP32   54.0%          --                 109              --

The policy survives quantization: -8.0 points for a ~3x size reduction, and
the INT8 model still beats the FP32 artifact this campaign started from by
+27.0 points. Time-to-grasp on successful episodes is essentially unchanged
(26 -> 28 steps); the all-episode mean rises (45 -> 61) purely because more
episodes now run to the 200-step cap.

NUMERICAL AGREEMENT vs the FP32 teacher, 512 real states
  mean |delta| 0.1132   corr 0.941   max |delta| 2.0000
The max is a full-scale sign flip on at least one action dimension -- the
per-channel/ESP-NN symptom described in Sec 3 -- which is why per-tensor
weights are used and why behavioural evaluation, not output diff, is the
acceptance test.

TOOLCHAIN NOTES (litert-torch 0.9.3, TF 2.21, torch 2.11)
The documented single-shot path no longer exists and two of its successors
silently produce wrong artifacts:

  * convert() lost _ai_edge_converter_flags; TFLite converter flags are gone.
  * pt2e + convert_pt2e(fold_quantize=False) converts cleanly, validates, and
    reports a 0.00000 output diff -- while remaining FP32 (26.8 KB, 22 float32
    tensors). It looks like a success and is not quantized. ALWAYS check
    tensor dtypes, never the exit status.
  * pt2e + fold_quantize=True emits bare int8 tensors that the stablehlo
    lowering rejects ('must be ranked tensor of per-tensor integer quantized
    ... but got tensor<64x46xi8>').

Working path, now in qat_and_convert.py: convert to float32 .tflite first,
then apply ai-edge-quantizer's static_wi8_ai8 recipe with weight granularity
overridden from its CHANNELWISE default to TENSORWISE, calibrated on real
states. The converter now fails loudly if no int8 tensors are produced.

Built in an isolated venv (/home/fahim/Thesis_fahim/convert_venv) that inherits
torch from the training venv via a .pth link, so the robosuite/MuJoCo
environment is untouched.

CAVEAT
Seed 0 only, and single-seed INT8 numbers carry the same ~5-point SE as the
FP32 ones. The FP32 cross-seed mean for this arm is 80.7% (range 76-89), so
the -8.0 quantization cost is measured against that arm's best seed.
